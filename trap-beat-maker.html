FAQ

What BPM are trap beats?
Trap usually runs 130–150 BPM (often felt as a half-time 65–75). thebeatsynth's trap starter loads at 140 BPM.
Can I program 808 slides?
Yes — the 808 instrument supports pitch glide between notes, directly in the piano roll.
