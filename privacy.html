Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

thebeatsynth is a beat-making app that runs in your browser. It doesn't require an account, and it doesn't store any beat, sound, or project you make anywhere but your own device. This page explains exactly what that means.

What stays on your device

Your projects, patterns, imported audio, and app settings are stored locally in your browser, using localStorage and IndexedDB. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, ever — we have no server-side database for project data, and nothing you create is uploaded anywhere.

This also means your beats live in that one browser, on that one device. Clearing your browser's site data, using a different browser, or switching devices will not bring them with you — there's nothing to "sync," because nothing is stored on a server in the first place. If you want to move a project, use File → ZIP to save it as a file you can open elsewhere.

Sharing a link

Create link and Embed code (under Library → Share) build a link that contains your entire beat, compressed and encoded directly into the URL itself. Nothing is uploaded to our server to make this work — the link's recipient downloads it straight from wherever you send it, and their browser decodes it locally. We never see it and never store it.

(Earlier versions of thebeatsynth had a Gallery and a server-stored "Short link" feature that did upload a copy of your project. Both have been removed — we no longer store any music, pattern, or project data on our server, full stop.)

Export

Export (WAV, MP3, MIDI, stems, video) renders and downloads a file straight to your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Microphone and MIDI

Hum to notes asks for microphone access to listen to your pitch and turn it into notes in the piano roll. That audio is processed entirely in your browser — it is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Connecting a MIDI controller uses your browser's Web MIDI API to talk to your hardware directly. This communication stays on your device.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see how many people visit the site. It's cookie-free and doesn't track you individually — it reports aggregated, anonymized numbers like page views and approximate country, not anything tied to you personally.

Sound libraries

Built-in instrument sounds are loaded on demand from two third-party content hosts: gleitz.github.io (GitHub Pages) and cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr). Loading a file from any website involves an ordinary HTTP request, which incidentally exposes your IP address to that host, the same as loading an image from any site would. We don't control what those hosts do with that request, but no project data or personal information is included in it.

Cookies

thebeatsynth doesn't set cookies. App data is kept in localStorage and IndexedDB as described above.

Children's privacy

thebeatsynth doesn't ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There's no account system and no form that asks who you are.

Data retention and deletion

Local data is yours to clear at any time, by clearing your browser's site data for thebeatsynth.com, or using "New Project" in the app. Since we don't store any project or audio data on our server, there's nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
