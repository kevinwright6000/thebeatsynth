FAQ

What BPM is phonk?
Phonk commonly runs 125–135 BPM. thebeatsynth's starter loads at 130 BPM.
How do I get the distorted 808 sound?
The 808 channel's FX chain includes drive and crush — push those further than the starter preset for more distortion.
