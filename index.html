Hum to notes

Press Record, then sing or hum one note at a time into your microphone. After the count-in it listens for one pattern length and writes what you sang onto the piano roll of . Wear headphones so the clicks do not leak into the mic.

Progression and bass
Chord progression for
Chords follow your project key and scale, so change those first. The chord names come from the scale: the same numbers give major chords in a major key and minor ones in a minor key.
Bass from chords
Reads the chords on the channel you pick, in the current pattern, and writes a bass line that changes with them. Run it again after you edit the chords.
