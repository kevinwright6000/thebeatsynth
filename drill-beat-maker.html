FAQ

What BPM is drill?
Drill is usually written around 140–145 BPM (often felt in half-time). thebeatsynth's drill starter loads at 142 BPM.
How do I get the sliding 808 sound?
The 808 instrument supports pitch glide between notes — drag note pitches in the piano roll and the slide happens automatically.
